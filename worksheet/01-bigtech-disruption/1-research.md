# 1 - Tu nghien cuu: tim 1 case bi big tech AI anh huong + so lieu that

## Buoc 0 - Chon case

- **Ten case**: Chegg
- **Big tech AI tao ap luc**: ChatGPT / GPT-4 cua OpenAI
- **Ly do chon**: Chegg la case de nhat vi co day du chuoi du lieu cong khai: ngay ChatGPT ra mat, phan ung cua ban lanh dao Chegg, doanh thu, subscriber, sa thai, doi thu phan ung nhanh hon, va ca tin hieu ve distribution collapse.

---

## Phan A - 4 nhom so lieu can tim

### Nhom 1 - Quy mo truoc va sau

- Truoc AI shock, Chegg van la nen tang hoc tap co quy mo lon: **8.2M subscribers** va **$766.9M doanh thu nam 2022**.
- Sau AI shock, quy mo giam ro: **6.6M subscribers** va **$617.6M doanh thu nam 2024**; rieng **Q4/2024 con 3.6M subscribers**.

### Nhom 2 - Moc thoi gian big tech AI ra tinh nang tuong tu

- **30/11/2022**: OpenAI mo ChatGPT cho cong chung.
- **01/2023**: ChatGPT dat **100M monthly active users** chi sau 2 thang.
- **14/03/2023**: GPT-4 ra mat, tang them chat luong reasoning cua san pham thay the.

### Nhom 3 - Phan ung cua Chegg sau khi ChatGPT bung no

- **17/04/2023**: Chegg cong bo **CheggMate** xay tren **GPT-4**.
- **01/05/2023**: Chegg thua nhan tu thang 3 da thay "significant spike" trong student interest voi ChatGPT va no dang anh huong den new customer growth.
- **17/06/2024**: Chegg cong bo tai cau truc, cat **441 nhan vien**, tuong duong **23% workforce**.

### Nhom 4 - Doi thu AI thay the

- **Quizlet** la doi thu phan ung nhanh hon: **Q-Chat** ra mat ngay **01/03/2023**, som hon CheggMate 47 ngay.
- Quizlet co san **60M nguoi dung hang thang**, nen phat tan AI tutor ngay trong mot distribution channel da co.

## Phan D - Phat hien ban dau

- ChatGPT khong can doi den 2024 moi gay tac dong: chi **5 thang sau ngay mo public**, Chegg da noi ro ChatGPT dang lam hai **new customer growth** (S-03, S-05).
- Chegg co phan ung kha nhanh tren giay to khi ra **CheggMate** ngay 17/04/2023, nhung doi thu **Quizlet** con nhanh hon va co san **60M MAU** de phan pho AI tutor (S-06, S-08, S-09).
- Tac dong khong chi o cam nhan thi truong: subscriber giam tu **8.2M (2022)** xuong **6.6M (2024)** va doanh thu giam tu **$766.9M** xuong **$617.6M** (S-01, S-02, S-15).
- Moat cua Chegg co data va content, nhung distribution moat yeu: non-subscriber traffic giam manh trong 2024 cho thay top-of-funnel dang bi ChatGPT va AI Overviews rut mat (S-14).
- Thi truong von nhan dien fit collapse som hon bao cao nam: co phieu Chegg bi danh **-48.41% trong 1 ngay** sau canh bao ChatGPT (S-07).
